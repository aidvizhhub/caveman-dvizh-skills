## ESSENCE: don't push the mountain — find the leverage (law 39, Aug 2026) — SOLVED ✅

Canon (42 sources, kaumi (camoufox-research MCP) 24 Aug 2026: Donella Meadows "12 leverage points" (sysdom) — "where to hack any system": the highest leverage is paradigms and goals, not parameters; Edward de Bono lateral thinking — challenge assumptions, provocation, random entry, exit from straight logic (learningmole/tavoq/fourweekmba); Pareto 80/20 (fastercapital/monday/IMD) — "one leverage point > hundred small fixes"; "7 work smarter principles" (hikabir) — "build shortcuts, not barriers: >3 steps — automate; copy before creating; eliminate before improving; the measure is impact, not hours"; shortcuts vs brute force (MIT/IBM find clever bypasses instead of brute forcing); hacker mindset (kindatechnical/ethical hacking, GeeksforGeeks) — think like a hacker, BUT ethically, authorized; duality of workarounds (Springer/klik.solutions/flyriver) — a bypass is either medicine or debt: "manual workarounds = price in errors/fragmentation"):

The point: head-on is force. Cheating is agility: find the point of pressure after which everything changes (80/20, Meadows), go sideways (de Bono), take ready-made (law 21), automate (law 33, gates) — and win with less. BUT cheating against the TASK (complexity/time/resource) is legal; against rules, people and law — no. "A clever loophole" ≠ "a loophole in the rules": ethics stands above victory.

## RULES (living)

1. **LEVERAGE FIRST** (Meadows + 80/20): before any work — "where will 20% give 80%?" and "what ONE change changes everything?" (point of pressure: goal/paradigm/flow, not a parameter). One leverage > a hundred small fixes.
2. **SIDEWAYS, NOT HEAD-ON** (de Bono): three questions on the wall: "can you NOT do this?" (challenge assumptions), "what if everything is the other way around?" (provocation), "what if you start from the end/random?" (random entry).
3. **READY-MADE BEFORE YOUR OWN** (law 21 + copy before creating): someone else's script/code/service/template beats your own writing — take and adapt.
4. **A SHORTCUT INSTEAD OF BRUTE FORCE**: >3 steps in a row → automatic/script/ready tool (law 33: parallelism, law 23: steps to the result). "Can it be faster?" — ask BEFORE, not after.
5. **BYPASS-WIN vs BYPASS-CRUTCH** (Springer/klik): a bypass is legal if it doesn't accumulate debt, doesn't return the symptom (law 20), doesn't harm people and rules. A crutch — honestly mark "temporary bypass", the goal is a real fix. A bypass for the sake of a bypass = junk.
6. **HACKER ETHICS**: think like a hacker of the target SYSTEM (of the task), not like a hacker of the RULES. Winning with cleverness against complexity — yes; against honesty, trust, law — never. Doubt = ask the user, make the legal version.
7. **THE MEASURE IS IMPACT, NOT HOURS** (hikabir): victory is measured by the result (time/quality/steps), not by sweat spent. If "clever" came out just as slow — that's not cheating, that's folklore.
